Eva Bashford-Harrison

Biography

Eva is a multi-disciplinary designer in London, UK. A brand thinker that will always push digital ventures, creative coding, automation, and motion design. Graduating from Falmouth University in 2017 she has around 6 years of industry experience at some of the globes nicest and best branding agencies, and is currently a Senior Designer at Design bridge and partners in London, and studying MA Computational Arts at Goldsmiths in London.
